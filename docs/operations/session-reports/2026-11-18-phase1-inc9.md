# Session report: Phase 1, increment 9 (2026-11-18)

## Delivered
- V9 migration: provider payments can target a loan (`LOAN_REPAYMENT`); repayment channel
  `SUSPENSE`; `payments.suspense_resolution` with database maker-checker and finality;
  `payments.open_suspense_item` view (age in days, pending resolution).
- `LoanRepaymentService`: real allocation by product order over due installments and penalties,
  interest split between receivable (accrued) and income (not yet accrued), overpayment liability,
  outbox event.
- Paybill receipts quoting a loan number repay the loan; validation accepts ACTIVE loan numbers.
- Suspense API: `GET /api/v1/payments/suspense/items`, `POST .../items/{paymentId}/resolutions`
  (OPERATIONS), `POST .../resolutions/{id}/approve|reject` (SUPERVISOR, not the requester).
- Accounting `JournalPosting` port so lending does not depend on accounting persistence.
- OpenAPI contract: 4 paths and 4 schemas added, nothing existing changed.
- ADR-0010.
