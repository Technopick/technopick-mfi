# Session report: Phase 1, increment 44 (2026-11-26)

## Delivered
- V41: guarantee settings (recoveries clearing account, days past due, release as repaid), a
  current lock and amount recovered on each guarantee, guarantor calls with lines, and database
  rules: a lock only goes down, on a new matching hold, while the loan is active; recovered rises
  only through an approved call; a call needs an active loan at least the set days past due at the
  last close and at most what is owed; every approved line has its savings-to-clearing transfer and
  its INTERNAL repayment from clearing; nothing else posts to the clearing account. Limits, own
  cover and member exit now use the current lock.
- Core: GuarantorCallService and API (settings, a loan's guarantees and what can be called,
  request, approve, reject, list); end of day lowers locks to each pledge's share of the principal
  still outstanding when release as repaid is on.
- Staff app: a "Guarantors" panel on the loan page (days past due, locks, owed, what can be called,
  ask for a call) and a "Guarantor calls" page (decide calls; finance sets them up). ADR-0044.
