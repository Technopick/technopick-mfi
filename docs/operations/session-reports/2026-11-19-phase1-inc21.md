# Session report: Phase 1, increment 21 (2026-11-19)

## Delivered
- V20: loan closure record and checks; credit held per loan; credit application capped by the
  database; repayments checked against loan status when recorded.
- `LoanClosureService`: closes a loan after the repayment that finishes it, and during end of day.
- End of day applies overpaid credit to installments as they fall due, then closes repaid loans.
- Portfolio-at-risk report API and staff app page (PAR by branch, loans in arrears, closed loans
  holding credit). End of day result shows loans closed and credit applied.
- ADR-0021.

## Gap found and closed
Money paid ahead of an installment was held on the overpayment account and never applied, so a
customer who paid early would have gone into arrears when the installment fell due.
