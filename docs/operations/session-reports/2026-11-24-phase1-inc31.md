# Session report: Phase 1, increment 31 (2026-11-24)

## Delivered
- V28: interest in suspense (Reg 42). Per-tenant switch and suspense account (an unused asset
  account, which then becomes a control account). Append-only per-loan movements, each checked
  against its journal entry and kept between zero and the loan's unpaid interest. Nothing else may
  post to the suspense account. New reconciliation check `LOAN_INTEREST_SUSPENSE`. Database tests
  Z1-Z4.
- At end of day a substandard-or-worse loan's suspense is brought to its unpaid accrued interest
  and a performing loan's to zero. Paid interest becomes income at that day's close. Closure
  releases suspense to income; write-off releases it against the write-off expense.
- The prudential return shows interest in suspense and any non-performing interest still in
  income. The settings page has the switch and account; the reconciliation page labels the new
  check. ADR-0031.

## Issues found and fixed
- The test's first database run used a variable named like a column, and expected a trigger
  error where the missing UPDATE grant already refuses the change. Both fixed, and test Z3 now
  fails loudly if tenant A has no loan with unpaid interest, instead of skipping.
- The API test expected the wrong outcome for a loan payment by M-Pesa (`LOAN_REPAYMENT`).

## Test evidence (CI run 45354841290 on e1b6787, all seven jobs green)
- API test on the hand-worked TEST-ONLY book:
  - Switching on without an account is refused (400), and so is using a mapped bank account (422).
  - Nothing moves until the next close. At the 29 Nov close each of the four substandard loans
    holds exactly its unpaid interest in suspense and performing loans hold nothing.
  - Interest income equals all interest accrued less interest in suspense, and so does the suspense
    account. The recorded reconciliation shows 0.00 difference over 4 loans. The return reports
    the suspense and 0.00 of non-performing interest in income, and the suspense account is not
    listed as unmapped.
  - A 700.00 M-Pesa payment on a suspended loan lowers its suspense by the interest it paid (plus
    the day's accrual) at the 30 Nov close, and income rises.
  - Writing off a suspended loan releases its suspense against the write-off expense, which equals
    the amount written off less the suspended interest. Interest income is unchanged.
  - Switching off releases everything at the next close.
