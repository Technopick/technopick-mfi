# Session report: Phase 1, increment 42 (2026-11-26)

## Delivered
- V39: `membership` schema (exit settings, exit requests, settled accounts, refund payments) and a
  final EXITED customer status, with the rules enforced in the database: notice period copied and
  served, one open request, maker-checker, nothing outstanding (`membership.blockers`), settlement
  of each savings account's balance and unposted interest less tax checked against the ledger and
  the entry line by line, accounts closed, EXITED only through an approved exit and never changed,
  refund paid once in full from a non-control ASSET account with maker-checker, and nothing else
  posting to member refunds payable.
- Core: member exit service and API (`/api/v1/member-exits`): settings (finance), a member's
  position (what stops them leaving and the refund today), request, cancel, reject, approve, list,
  refund payment record and decision.
- Reconciliation: new MEMBER_REFUNDS_PAYABLE check; SAVINGS_INTEREST_PAYABLE now accounts for
  interest settled at exit.
- Staff app: "Members leaving" page (position with blockers and refund breakdown, request, exit
  requests with approve/reject/cancel, refund recording and approval, settings). ADR-0042.
- Browser journey: finance sets exit settings; the member holding shares is told to transfer them
  first; a new member with nothing outstanding asks to leave and the supervisor approves.

## Issues found and fixed
- The settled-balance rule first compared against the ledger balance after the exit entry, which
  forced an awkward order of writes; it now uses the balance before the exit's own entry.
- An untested claim about M-Pesa receipts to closed accounts was removed from the ADR.
- No CI failures beyond the expected API contract update.
