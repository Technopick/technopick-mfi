# Session report: Phase 1, increment 41 (2026-11-26)

## Delivered
- V38: `termdep` schema (products, deposits, accrual batches and rows, early withdrawal requests,
  closures) with the rules enforced in the database: product account classes, terms copied onto
  each deposit, placement on the business date within limits, exact placement entries, nightly
  interest arithmetic and batch totals, accruals and maturities only inside a running end of day,
  closure arithmetic and entries line by line, rollovers, maker-checker on early withdrawal,
  immutability, no direct postings to deposits or interest payable, and no closing a savings account
  that a deposit pays to.
- Core: fixed deposit service and API (`/api/v1/fixed-deposits`): products (finance), member
  deposits, deposit detail with what it pays at maturity and if withdrawn today, placement from the
  member's own savings, maturity instruction (pay out, roll over principal, roll over all), early
  withdrawal with maker-checker, early withdrawal list, portfolio and deposits maturing soon.
- End of day: new step after savings interest: accrue interest per product and branch, then close
  every deposit maturing on the next date (payout or rollover at current terms; payout if the
  product is retired or the member or account is no longer active). Summary and step timing added.
- Reconciliation: TERM_DEPOSIT_PRINCIPAL and TERM_DEPOSIT_INTEREST, live and at every close.
- Staff app: "Fixed deposits" page (find a member, place a deposit, deposit detail, change maturity
  instruction, ask to withdraw early, decide early withdrawals, holdings and maturing list with CSV,
  products with a finance form). ADR-0041.
- Browser journey: the teller-day journey now has finance create a product, the teller place
  10,000.00 from the member's savings, a loan officer ask to withdraw it early and the supervisor
  approve it, with the money back in savings; its closing reconciliation covers fixed deposits.

## Issues found and fixed
