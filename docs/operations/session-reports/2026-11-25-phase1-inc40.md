# Session report: Phase 1, increment 40 (2026-11-25)

## Delivered
- V37: `shares` schema (settings, register of movements, transfers, dividend runs and lines) with
  the rules enforced in the database: whole shares, own active savings account, active member, no
  holding below the minimum, purchase entries matching the movement exactly, transfers only as an
  approved balanced pair, dividend lines matching the register and the arithmetic, run totals
  matching the entry, record date closed, and nothing else posting to share capital.
- Core: share service and API (`/api/v1/shares`): settings (finance), member holding and history,
  register, purchase from the member's own savings, transfer with maker-checker, dividend preview,
  request and maker-checker approval that posts gross, tax, payable and each member's net. ADR-0040.
- Reconciliation: new SHARE_CAPITAL check (ledger equals register), live and at end of day; the
  reconciliation page also labels the IFRS 9 allowance check.
- Staff app: "Member shares" page (find a member, buy, ask for a transfer, decide transfers,
  register with CSV download) and "Dividends" page (calculate, request, approve or reject, download
  members, share settings).
- Browser journey: the savings journey now has finance set up shares, the teller buy 5,000.00 from
  the member's savings, a loan officer ask to transfer 2,000.00 and a supervisor approve it; the
  journey's closing reconciliation (every control account matches) now covers share capital.

## Issues found and fixed
- Constraint trigger names clashed with PostgreSQL's automatic CHECK constraint names
  (`<table>_check`); renamed.
- In the browser journey, a register-row selector also matched the transfer row; narrowed to the
  register's branch column.
